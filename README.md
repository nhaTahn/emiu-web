# em iu.

Published static website: https://nhatahn.github.io/emiu-web/

Flower and gift choices stay in the visitor’s browser until Google Sheets sync is configured. When enabled, confirmed choices and names are sent to the owner’s private Sheet; uploads and avatars are excluded. The visitor can also copy or share the recap. Photos added on the page stay on that device; the included album is served with the website.
